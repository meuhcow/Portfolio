RSAC student experience

Attending RSAC as a university student was honestly one of those experiences that puts everything into perspective.

You go from learning concepts in class to suddenly being surrounded by people actually building, breaking, and defending real systems at scale. It made everything feel a lot more real and showed me how much there still is to learn.

I got to explore new ideas, talk to people in the field, and challenge myself through security games and real-world scenarios. It pushed me out of my comfort zone in a new city.

Grateful for the opportunity and excited for what's ahead. Huge thank you to D3 Security for making this possible.
